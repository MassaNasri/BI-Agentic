## Release evidence record template

For each CAP and gate: `status`, `owner`, `reviewer`, `commit`, `image_digest`, `environment`, `UTC_timestamp`, `test_or_drill_run_link`, `expected`, `observed`, `limitations`, `rollback_reference`. Current future gates are **NOT RUN**, even where design or unit helpers exist. CI on another SHA or a local fixture cannot fill a deployment evidence cell.

The excluded features are Structured & Unstructured RAG, Proactive Monitoring Agent, and Arabic & Voice Support expansion: **OUT OF JANUARY 10 RELEASE SCOPE**. Historical mentions and maintained source are classified in 48; there is no milestone or hidden acceptance gate for their expansion.

**Foundation-C Local Engineering: CLOSED. Foundation-C External Deployment Validation: DEFERRED_TO_PRODUCTION_READINESS. Production Readiness: NO-GO.**

Foundation-D local implementation requires Foundation-C Local Engineering CLOSED, the required current release CI GREEN, and no A/B/C local-security regression.
